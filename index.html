ABOUT US

Founded in 2019, we are a Manila-based interior design firm specializing in residential and commercial projects. We create thoughtful, refined spaces that balance functionality, materiality, and timeless design.

From concept development to detailed design and project execution, we work closely with our clients to transform ideas into spaces that are both distinctive and purposeful.

Our approach is rooted in understanding each project's unique character—delivering interiors that are carefully considered, highly functional, and built to stand the test of time.

02

WHAT SETS US APART

At Method, we believe that great design is defined not only by the outcome, but by the process behind it.

Our name embodies this philosophy: Method = Process. From the initial concept and planning through to implementation, every stage is guided and executed by one dedicated team. This hands-on approach allows us to remain deeply involved in every decision, detail, and execution throughout the life of a project.

By bringing design, planning, and implementation together under one roof, we create a seamless connection between vision and reality—ensuring that every space is delivered with clarity, consistency, and intention.

One team. One process. One vision.

This is our Method.
